THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SCENE

Simple geometry. Real solar motion.

SUNSPACE is an educational sun–shadow laboratory for landscape architecture, planning and architecture. Follow the loop: observe → model → analyse → evaluate → design → compare.

Solar position

Sun direction is calculated from latitude, longitude, date and local time using NOAA’s approximate solar equations. The selected time zone handles daylight saving. Night is shown separately and never counted as shade.

Spatial analysis

Building prisms, opaque tree crowns and solid pergola roofs cast geometric shadows onto flat ground. Coverage samples open ground on a 3 m grid, excluding building footprints. Average shade and the heatmap sample 08:00–18:00 at 30-minute midpoints, counting only daylight. Before and after share the same camera and solar settings.

Data & assumptions

The Barcelona preset uses an included OpenStreetMap snapshot from 14 September 2026. Other live areas use OpenStreetMap building ways and mapped trees, retrieved through Overpass. Heights tagged in metres or feet are retained; levels are estimated at 3 m per floor; missing heights default to 12 m. Complex building relations, terrain, materials and seasonal leaf loss are not modelled. Buildings outside the study area may contribute shadows.

Trees use opaque ellipsoidal crowns (8 m wide, 4–10 m high); pergolas use opaque 8 × 6 m roofs at 3 m. Results indicate geometric solar obstruction, not temperature, thermal comfort or certified environmental performance.
